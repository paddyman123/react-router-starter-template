# StoneMatch — 30-Day Enquiry Campaign

## Core proposition
**Before you buy it. StoneMatch it.**

Primary offer: **The Free StoneMatch Quote Check**

Already have a worktop quote? Send StoneMatch the supplier, material/specification and price. We help the homeowner understand whether the overall deal makes sense. If it looks strong, say so. If there may be a better route, explain the alternative.

Primary CTA: **DM “CHECK”**
Secondary CTA: **Check my worktop quote** on stonematch.co.uk

## Content rules
- Lead with a homeowner question/problem, not a product photograph.
- Reels: hook immediately, one idea per video, natural face-to-camera delivery, subtitles, finished kitchen/stone B-roll where useful.
- Carousels: educational, saveable and shareable.
- Avoid claiming StoneMatch is always cheapest. Position around value, specification, suitability and confidence.
- Every conversion post has one CTA only.
- Use real anonymised quote/project examples wherever permission and data allow.

## Week 1 — Create the problem
### Reel 1 — Launch the Quote Check
Hook: “Already got a quote for your kitchen worktops? Don’t pay the deposit yet.”
Body: “I’m Patrick from StoneMatch. Send us the material, specification and price and we’ll help you sense-check the deal. If it looks good, we’ll tell you. If we think there’s a better route, we’ll explain why.”
CTA: “DM CHECK.”

### Carousel 1 — 5 things to check before accepting a worktop quote
1. Cover: Before you accept your worktop quote…
2. Is the exact material/brand clear?
3. Are thickness and edge details comparable?
4. What fabrication/cut-outs are included?
5. Is templating and installation included?
6. What happens if something goes wrong?
7. Want a second pair of eyes? DM CHECK.

### Reel 2 — High-street myth
Hook: “Buying a new kitchen? You don’t automatically have to buy the worktops from the same company.”
Explain that homeowners can compare the worktop element independently and should compare like-for-like.
CTA: “Before you commit, StoneMatch it. DM CHECK.”

## Week 2 — Build authority
### Reel 3 — Quartz vs porcelain
Hook: “Quartz or porcelain? Here’s how I’d choose.”
Give three concise decision factors: look, practical requirements, budget/specification.
CTA: “Not sure what suits your kitchen? DM MATCH.”

### Carousel 2 — Why two ‘same’ quotes can be different
Cover: “Both say quartz. So why is one £1,500 more?”
Slides: material/brand; slab usage; edge work; cut-outs; vein/pattern work; templating/fitting; service/aftercare.
CTA: “Compare the specification, not just the total. DM CHECK.”

### Reel 4 — Insider advice
Hook: “Three questions I’d ask before ordering any stone worktop.”
Questions: exact material; exactly what is included; who templates/fabricates/installs and what aftercare is provided.
CTA: “Save this before you order.”

## Week 3 — Demonstrate the service
### Reel 5 — Anonymised Quote Check
Hook on screen: “This homeowner had already been quoted £X…”
Show anonymised project details and explain what StoneMatch checked. Never manufacture a saving; show the genuine outcome, including cases where the original deal was good.
CTA: “Got a quote? DM CHECK.”

### Carousel 3 — What happens when you StoneMatch a quote?
1. Send the quote/project details.
2. We understand the brief.
3. We check material/specification and inclusions.
4. We consider suitable fabricator/options.
5. You decide with more information.
6. Free for homeowners. No obligation.

### Reel 6 — Price objection
Hook: “The cheapest worktop quote isn’t necessarily the best deal.”
Explain one or two specification/service differences that can change value.
CTA: “Want help comparing yours? DM CHECK.”

## Week 4 — Conversion push
### Reel 7 — Before/after project story
Hook: “They knew the kitchen they wanted — but not where to buy the worktops.”
Tell a real StoneMatch journey in 20–30 seconds.
CTA: “Starting from scratch? DM MATCH.”

### Carousel 4 — Are you at one of these stages?
- Kitchen ordered, worktops not ordered.
- Worktop quote received.
- Colour saved but no supplier chosen.
- Comparing quartz/porcelain/granite/marble.
- Renovating but overwhelmed by options.
Final: “You’re ready to StoneMatch it.”

### Reel 8 — Direct response
Hook: “I’m not saying your worktop quote is expensive… but wouldn’t it make sense to check?”
Explain Free Quote Check in 15 seconds.
CTA: “DM CHECK or use the free quote check at stonematch.co.uk.”

## Stories — repeat weekly

- Question box: “What worktop material are you considering?”
- Quick quiz: Quartz / Porcelain / Granite / Marble
- Project/installation proof
- Face-to-camera tip from Patrick
- Quote Check reminder: “Got a quote sitting in your inbox? DM CHECK.”

## DM conversion script
When someone sends CHECK:
“Of course. Send me the quote or the main details you have — ideally the material/colour, price, rough plan or sizes and your postcode. I’ll use that to understand whether we’re comparing like-for-like and where StoneMatch may be able to help.”

After receiving details:
“Thanks — I’ll look at the overall specification rather than just the headline price. If what you’ve been offered already looks strong, I’ll tell you. If I think there’s another option worth considering, I’ll explain why.”

## What to measure every week
- Qualified enquiries
- DM CHECK conversations
- Website quote-check submissions
- Cost per qualified enquiry on paid campaigns
- Reel watch-through/retention
- Shares/sends and saves
- Enquiry-to-fabricator handover rate
- Handover-to-sale rate
- Average commission/revenue per converted lead

The campaign should be optimised for qualified enquiries and eventual sales, not follower count or likes.
